RULES

JOINING GAME CAPS MATCHMAKING RATING STRIKES RANKS LEAGUES

01JOINING THE LADDER

#
  • M'Hunters clan members are added automatically. An admin can add players from outside the clan.
  • You start at 1000 rating (Cobalt league, Recruit rank). Your first game is created on the next hourly run.

02GAME CAPS

#

Your game cap is the most ladder games you can have going at once.

  • Default is 2, max is 3.
  • Set it to 0 to take a break. You keep your rating.
  • Ask an admin to change your cap.

03MATCHMAKING

#

The ladder bot, Norman, runs every hour. It creates new games and records finished ones.

  • Active players are matched with other active players first.
  • Join new games within 3 days. If you don't, the game is deleted and you get a strike.
  • Declining a game also gives you a strike.

04RATING

#

Everyone starts at 1000. Each game moves your rating by up to 40 points.

  • Beating a higher-rated player earns more than beating a lower-rated one.
  • Draws (vote to end) don't change ratings.
  • Surrendering or getting booted counts as a loss.

05STRIKES & COMING BACK

#
  • 2 strikes in a row makes you inactive. You keep your rating.
  • Inactive players get 1 game at a time and wait 2 weeks between games. Each extra strike adds 2 weeks, up to 12.
  • To come back, join your next game. Once it starts, your strikes reset and you're active again.

06RANKS

#

Your rank goes up with ladder wins and never goes down.
